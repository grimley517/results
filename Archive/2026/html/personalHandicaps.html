How personal handicaps are calculated:

For each race in a rolling years worth of races (including Wednesday evenings but not short course) all the Portsmouth Yardstick corrected times are averaged and multiplied by 1.05 to give a cut off time any times greater than this value are discarded, because they are most likely the result of someone having a really bad race so is not representative and will unduly influence the handicaps.

Sample race:

CompetitorCorrected Time (s)
Ade3000
Bob3100
Carl3200
Drew3300
Earl4000

For out sample race:

  • Cut off = (3000+3100+3200+3300+4000)*1.05/5 = 3320
  • Average of all results bellow the cut off is = (3000+3100+3200+3300)/4 = 3150
  • Audit = (competiors corrected time / Race Average) * 1000 rounded to the nearest whole number
CompetitorAudit
Ade3000/3150*1000 = 952
Bob3100/3150*1000 = 984
Carl3200/3150*1000 = 1016
Drew3300/3150*1000 = 1048
Earl4000/3150*1000 = 1269

For each competitor the average of all there Audits is taken to determine their personal number, any audits greater than 1500 are not included in the average, to discard particularly bad races where a competitor has lost a lap due to being OCS or capsized a lot!

To use the personal handicap the formular is:

Personal correct time = PY corrected time / Personal handicap * 1000
